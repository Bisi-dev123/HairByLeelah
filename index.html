Hi, I'm Leelah.

Welcome to Hair By Leelah! I am a passionate hair artist and certified protective stylist serving Scarborough and the Greater Toronto Area.

My styling philosophy revolves around healthy hair longevity. Too often, clients associate braiding with tight tension and scalp headaches. My mission is the opposite: creating breathtaking, crisp, long-lasting styles with zero painful pulling and complete edge preservation.

Whether you want waist-length bohemian knotless braids, sleek cornrows, or a flawless lace wig melt for a special event, you can sit back in my private salon studio, enjoy your favorite show, and leave feeling confident and radiant.

Hair By Leelah • Studio Policies & Agreement

1. Deposit & Payment

A non-refundable 30% deposit is required to confirm every appointment. The remaining balance (70%) is due immediately upon completion of your hair service via Cash, Interac e-Transfer, Debit, or Credit card.

2. Cancellations & Notice Period

We respect your time and ask that you respect ours. Please provide at least 48 hours notice for any cancellations. Cancellations with less than 48 hours notice will forfeit the deposit.

3. Rescheduling Policy

Deposits are transferable one time to a new date within 30 days of the original appointment, provided notice is given at least 48 hours in advance. Less than 48 hours notice requires a new deposit.

4. Late Arrivals & No-Shows

There is a 15-minute grace period. At 15 minutes past your start time, a $15 late fee will be charged. At 20 minutes late, your appointment will be cancelled to protect the schedule of subsequent clients, and your deposit is non-refundable. No-shows forfeit deposit and will not be permitted to rebook.

5. Hair Preparation

Please come with your hair washed, thoroughly detangled, blown out straight, and free of heavy oils or butters (unless you select our Wash & Blowdry Add-On).

6. Stylist Cancellation Guarantee

In the event of an unavoidable emergency on our end, your deposit is 100% refunded or transferred with an additional $20 courtesy credit.
